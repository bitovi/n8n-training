Here’s a **genre-based chapter structure guide**

---

## 🧨 Action

1. **Introduction & Stakes** — Introduce protagonist, threat, and motivation.
2. **Trigger Event** — A violent or dramatic catalyst forces action.
3. **Chase or Conflict** — Fast-paced sequences test the hero’s resolve.
4. **Reversal** — Plans fail; allies or weapons are lost.
5. **Climax** — High-intensity confrontation with the main antagonist.
6. **Resolution** — Aftermath, survival, and setup for potential continuation.

---

## 🧭 Adventure

1. **Call to Adventure** — The hero learns of a quest or discovery.
2. **Crossing the Threshold** — Journey begins; new world or environment introduced.
3. **Trials & Companions** — Encounters with allies, obstacles, and lessons.
4. **Darkest Valley** — Hero faces internal doubt or external danger.
5. **Triumph & Discovery** — Main goal achieved through courage or teamwork.
6. **Return & Reflection** — The hero brings lessons or treasures back home.

---

## ⚔️ Action / Adventure

1. **Setup & Motivation** — Define the hero’s goal and the opposing force.
2. **Inciting Incident** — A sudden event demands action and exploration.
3. **Journey of Conflict** — Battles interspersed with exploration and moral tests.
4. **Revelation & Betrayal** — Twist or betrayal heightens urgency.
5. **Epic Showdown** — Combined physical and emotional climax.
6. **Restoration** — Balance restored, but scars remain.

---

## 😂 Comedy

1. **Ordinary Chaos** — Introduce flawed but relatable characters in everyday trouble.
2. **Escalation of Errors** — Small misunderstandings spiral out of control.
3. **Peak Absurdity** — Everything that can go wrong, does.
4. **Moment of Clarity** — The truth (or punchline) begins to surface.
5. **Resolution** — Problems resolved with humor and heart.
6. **Afterlaugh** — A playful epilogue or ironic callback.

---

## 🧙 Fantasy

1. **World & Prophecy** — Establish lore, powers, and destiny hints.
2. **Inciting Magic** — A magical event disturbs balance or awakens potential.
3. **Quest Assembly** — Allies gather; mentor guidance; first taste of magic’s cost.
4. **Temptation & Trial** — Hero faces moral or mystical test.
5. **Final Confrontation** — Battle between light and dark, often symbolic.
6. **New Order** — Restoration or transformation of the world (and hero).

---

## 🚀 Science Fiction

1. **Setting & Premise** — Introduce futuristic world and scientific hook.
2. **Disruption** — Technology, discovery, or anomaly upends the norm.
3. **Exploration & Discovery** — Characters probe the unknown—space, AI, or mind.
4. **Ethical Dilemma** — Science versus humanity tension peaks.
5. **Climax** — The experiment, war, or system breaks or evolves.
6. **Aftermath** — A new paradigm or haunting lesson emerges.
